Most recently I was a backend software engineer intern at VividSeats, the ticket marketplace, in Toronto, from January to April 2025. I worked in Java, on services that run on Kubernetes and AWS, and most of my work was around search.

A lot of our Algolia search traffic was for time-insensitive data, so it did not need a live query every time. I designed and built a multi-tiered SQLite caching layer in Java to offload those queries.

That reduced request latency from about 250 milliseconds to 30 at p99, and it improved backend throughput. To roll it out safely, I used Argo Rollouts for canary deployments on Kubernetes, with production traffic increasing gradually.

I also optimized our batch indexing. I restructured the pagination and the batching logic, and that cut CPU usage by over 60 percent, which improved our overall resource efficiency.
